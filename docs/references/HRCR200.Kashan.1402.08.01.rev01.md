# HRCR200 feasibility report — source audit

- Source document: `HRCR200.Kashan.1402.08.01. rev01.pdf`
- Report date: 1402/08/01
- Project: Hot Steel Coil Rolling Mill, nominal capacity 200,000 tonnes/year
- Source pages: printed pages 127–130 (PDF pages 138–141)

## Financing structure stated by the report

The narrative on printed page 129 explicitly states:
- Sponsor/shareholder equity: 70% of fixed investment.
- Long-term loan principal: 30% of fixed investment.
- Loan rate: 23% plus 5% issuance cost = 28% effective stated rate.
- Repayment: 5 years, quarterly, 20 instalments; first instalment in 1405 Q1.
- Construction period: 2 years (1403–1404); production starts 1405.
- Working capital in the first production year is funded by shareholders; later increases are funded from project cash flow.
- Discount rates: 25% for total project investment and 30% for shareholder equity.

Amounts shown in the report (million IRR):
- Construction-period investment before capitalized interest: 18,702,835.
- Shareholder equity: 13,091,985.
- Loan principal: 5,610,851.
- Capitalized construction-period loan interest: 3,298,633.
- Total investment including capitalized interest: 22,001,468 (as printed).

The equity and loan principal amounts independently reconcile to approximately 70% / 30% of 18,702,835.

## Source inconsistencies to preserve, not silently correct

1. The financing narrative on printed page 129 says loan principal is 30% of fixed investment and gives 5,610,851, which mathematically agrees with 30%.
2. The funding table on printed page 130 labels that same 5,610,851 loan amount as 20% of construction investment. This percentage conflicts with both the narrative and the amount; treat it as a likely report typo and use the 70/30 amount-based structure for the benchmark.
3. The funding table's rounded shares are 60% equity, 26% loan principal, and 15% capitalized interest when measured against gross sources including capitalized interest. These are not the underlying equity/debt split. Excluding capitalized interest, the core fixed-investment financing is 70% equity / 30% loan.
4. Printed totals have 1-million-IRR rounding/arithmetic differences: 18,463,009 + 239,827 = 18,702,836, while the report prints 18,702,835; and 13,091,985 + 5,610,851 + 3,298,633 = 22,001,469, while the report prints 22,001,468. Preserve original source values and flag the differences during reconciliation.
