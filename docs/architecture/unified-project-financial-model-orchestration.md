# Unified project financial-model orchestration

`calculateProjectFinancialModelFromDrafts` is the shared form-to-engine entry point for a greenfield feasibility model and for existing enterprises that enable the detailed opening balance sheet. It executes the following ordered chain:

1. Product sales and production-cost forms -> sales revenue, fixed/variable costs, production quantity and finished-goods roll-forward.
2. Working-capital component schedules -> net working-capital balances and period changes. Explicit balances are supported for every component; a 360-day days-coverage/turnover driver is also supported for value-mode trade receivables valued on sales revenue. Driver-derived balances use the project's fiscal-year sales revenue, and any simultaneously entered balance must reconcile to the derived amount. Other category/basis/activity combinations remain blocked rather than approximated.
3. Fixed-asset acquisition vintages and pre-production expenditure vintages -> period depreciation/amortization, annual depreciation, fixed CAPEX, pre-production cash expenditure and disposal schedule.
4. Loan facility forms -> periodic debt schedules, foreign-currency remeasurement and linked three-statement posting.

The orchestration is fail-fast. An invalid or incomplete upstream schedule prevents a downstream result from being presented as valid. It does not silently fall back to a parallel cash-flow engine.

The wizard passes the saved project financial forms and project foundation into the periodic calculation panel. Opening finished-goods quantity and opening balances for each working-capital component are visible form inputs. Existing enterprises can enable the detailed opening balance sheet: cash, current-asset and current-liability components, gross fixed assets and accumulated depreciation, deferred pre-operating assets, short- and long-term debt, paid-in capital and retained earnings. The opening form is rejected unless every required balance is non-negative and the accounting equation reconciles within 0.01 currency units. Net working capital, net fixed assets and total opening debt are derived from the detailed entries and passed to the statement engine. The tax treatment of foreign-exchange losses is selected in the project foundation.

## Current constraints

- Existing-enterprise orchestration is enabled only when the detailed opening balance sheet is explicitly activated and reconciled. A blank or unbalanced opening statement is rejected; it is never silently replaced with zero balances.
- Facility fee amounts use the explicit fee date, fee type, currency, commitment limit and expense/capitalization treatment; fee bases are calculated from the preliminary principal schedule before fee posting.
- Repayment frequency must match project period frequency.
- Foreign exchange uses the period rate for aggregated movements and closing remeasurement.
- Asset acquisition is dated at period-end because the form records acquisition periods rather than exact dates; disposal losses and multi-vintage disposal require additional accounting detail.
- Indirect production costs require an explicit allocation basis before allocation to product cost.
- The raw-template pre-production expenditure section is bound through the same dated asset-lifecycle adapter as fixed assets, but is tagged as pre-production so its acquisition is posted to pre-operating expenditure rather than fixed CAPEX. Its amortization method, life, start rule, first-year months and salvage percentage are explicit inputs. The adapter rejects missing or inconsistent acquisition values and exchange rates.

A calculation-coverage guard blocks any populated raw-template section or partially mapped field that does not yet have a periodic-engine posting. User-defined indirect-cost allocation percentages are now bound to product cost rows and must reconcile to exactly 100% per allocation key. A cost row references one allocation-row ID; rows sharing the same key name and cost centre form the complete allocation group. Formula-based allocation types (for example, direct-cost or sales-revenue drivers) remain blocked until their drivers are implemented. Paid-in ordinary-equity contributions are mapped by period and currency into the engine's equity funding schedule. Signed tax-only depreciation adjustments are also mapped by period and currency; they change taxable income without changing accounting depreciation. Signed annual income-tax adjustments are posted by project period to both leveraged and unleveraged tax payable (positive increases, negative deductions floored at zero); the tax currency must be the local accounting currency. Non-corporate tax regimes and foreign-currency tax settlement remain blocked. Grants/subsidies, capital refunds, preferred-share terms and joint-venture/equity classes remain blocked until dedicated accounting and distribution schedules are implemented. Profit distribution, separate starting-balance rows, economic-item/effect valuation, sensitivity inputs and incremental-analysis rows also remain blocked until dedicated calculation adapters are implemented. The guard is intentional: an entered value must never be silently omitted from a result labelled valid.

The production/sales adapter reconciles finished-goods quantities period by period, but it does not yet value opening or closing finished-goods inventory in currency or derive cost of goods sold from inventory movements. Therefore, when production-cost rows are present, the adapter now rejects any product with non-zero opening or closing finished-goods quantity. This fail-closed boundary prevents all production cost from being expensed while unsold inventory remains on hand, and prevents sales of opening inventory from being treated as if its carrying value were known. Costed inventory becomes eligible only after an explicit opening inventory valuation and a supported period-by-period cost-flow method are implemented and reconciled.

The working-capital days-coverage adapter currently supports sales-based receivables only. Production-based inventory coverage, unit-mode quantity valuation, cost-of-production/cost-of-sales drivers, initial stock quantity/value, recovery timing and cash-deposit interest still require dedicated source schedules. The external reference fixture currently verifies production, sales, finished-goods inventory and revenue for project periods 5–10. Full financial parity across all statements remains unverified.


Discount temporal reference points are now mapped to the first project period's start or end date. A temporal reference point and explicit reference date are mutually exclusive. Short NPV horizon remains blocked until a dedicated truncated-horizon metric is implemented.
